01 / What we do

Small voices.

03 / Our first chapter

The Founding Five.

PROPOSED NO-FEE PILOT PROGRAMME

We're designing five tightly scoped projects with Wellington-region organisations. The aim is to do useful work, listen carefully, improve our approach and earn credibility through results.

01 · Listen

Understand the challenge and agree on one achievable outcome.

02 · Build

Create a useful communications or strategy resource the organisation can put to work.

03 · Learn

Review what helped, what didn't and what should happen next.

Participation is not yet open. Projects will be selected with clear scope, capacity and ethical safeguards.
