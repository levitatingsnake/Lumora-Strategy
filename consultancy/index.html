PROPOSED NO-FEE PILOT PROGRAMME

We're designing five tightly scoped projects with Wellington-region organisations. The goal is simple: do useful work, listen carefully, improve our methods and earn credibility through results.

Participation is not yet open. Projects will be selected with clear scope, capacity and ethical safeguards.
